01 / About

Not just writing code. Building the foundation.

My engineering path started with Electrical & Electronics Engineering and evolved through software, embedded systems and automotive development.

Today my focus is platform software: the layer between vehicle-level signals and the software functions that consume them. I work with abstractions, interfaces, modes, state handling, integration and verification. The interesting problems are the ones where software has to behave correctly not only in the happy path, but across transitions, dependencies and system conditions.
